About

I am a postdoctoral research fellow at Chonnam National University, working with Jaegi Jeon. Previously I held postdoctoral positions at Sungkyunkwan University with Jeonggyu Huh and at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies with Minsuk Kwak. I received my Ph.D. in Financial Engineering from Ajou University under Hyeng Keun Koo.

My current work centers on continuous-time portfolio optimization with neural methods, including Pontryagin-guided policy optimization and physics-informed policy iteration, and on empirical asset pricing and generative modeling of financial data.
